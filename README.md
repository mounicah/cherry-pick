# Cherry-Pick Explained Like I'm 5 🍒

## What is Cherry-Pick?

Imagine you have a toy box (your `main` branch) with toys in order: 🚗, 🎮, 🧩, 🎨.

You also have a special box (your `feature-branch`) where you're building new toys: 🚀, 🧲, 🎸.

But oh no! Your friend realized that the 🧲 toy is SUPER cool and should be in the main toy box RIGHT NOW, even though you're still working on 🚀 and 🎸.

**Cherry-pick** is like reaching into your special box, grabbing just the 🧲 toy, and adding it to the main toy box. You don't move the whole special box—just that ONE toy!

---

## The Branches in This Repo

- **main** - The stable, finished story (like a published book)
- **feature-branch** - Where we're writing new cool stuff (work in progress)
- **bugfix-branch** - Where we fixed something important in the middle
